Answers

What is Quiet Light?

Quiet Light is a quiet place to write what's on your mind — and be met, not managed. It is a calm AI companion for reflection, with Quiet Light, Quiet Sun, and Quiet Moon. It is not a coach and not a clinic.

Is Quiet Light therapy?

No. Quiet Light is not therapy, not a medical device, and not a substitute for professional care.

Who makes Quiet Light?

Quiet Light is made by Dagaz Apps LLC, an independent app studio. Support is at support@quietlight.app.

Where can I get Quiet Light?

Quiet Light is free to download on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.
