Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2026

Last updated: October 3, 2026 · Version 1.2.0

This Privacy Policy describes how Dagaz Apps LLC (“Quiet Light,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the Quiet Light mobile application (iOS and Android) and this website at quietlight.app (together, the “Services”).

This document is a disclosure of our practices. Using the Services does not, by itself, constitute consent to processing where a different legal basis is required (for example, under the GDPR). Where the law requires consent, we will ask for it separately — including in-app acknowledgment of this policy.

Controller. Dagaz Apps LLC, a Florida limited liability company. Privacy and rights requests: support@quietlight.app. Studio site: dagazapps.com.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account details (email address, and any name or profile fields you enter)
  • Messages, prompts, and other inputs you submit during reflection sessions
  • Saved reflections, memory-related data, and conversation history (especially for signed-in and Premium users)
  • Support emails, account-deletion requests, and any feedback you send (including in-app ratings of a reply)
  • If you interact with our public pages on social platforms, whatever you choose to send there, plus any aggregate analytics the host provides to us

Information collected automatically

  • Device information (type, OS version, app version, platform)
  • IP address (used for daily usage limits, security, and abuse prevention)
  • Approximate location (country or region inferred from IP — we do not collect precise GPS)
  • App-generated identifiers (including a guest identifier when you use the App without an account)
  • Usage data needed to operate the App (session activity, crashes, feature use related to reflections and subscriptions)
  • Subscription and entitlement status via Apple, Google, and RevenueCat (we do not receive your full payment card number)

Website

quietlight.app is a static marketing site. We do not run advertising pixels or analytics scripts on this site, and we do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. The hosting provider may process standard server logs (IP address, user agent, pages requested) and strictly technical cookies to deliver the site and keep it secure. If you email us from a link on the site, we receive whatever you send.

Guest mode

You may use core features without creating an account. We still process the messages you send to generate responses, and we record your IP address and an app-generated guest identifier (when available) to apply daily limits and prevent abuse. We do not require an email address in guest mode.

Sensitive content you may choose to write

Quiet Light is not a health service and we do not ask you for medical information or other special-category data (for example racial or ethnic origin, religion, health, or biometric identifiers). Please do not include unnecessary personal information about yourself or others in reflections. We cannot control what you type. If you include it, that text is processed as user content to generate a reply and — if you have an account with history enabled — to store the thread. We do not use it for advertising. Treat reflections as private writing you choose to share with an AI system.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, personalize, and operate reflective responses
  • Maintain conversation continuity, saved reflections, and memory features for eligible users
  • Apply daily limits, prevent abuse, and keep the service secure
  • Process subscriptions, restore purchases, and manage Premium access
  • Send transactional email (for example, password reset, verification, or account notices) when you have an account
  • Improve reliability and understand aggregate product use
  • Comply with law and respond to your requests (including deletion)

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your reflections for advertising or for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not build advertising profiles. We may aggregate, pseudonymize, or de-identify information so it no longer identifies you, and use it to understand how the Services are used and to improve them. We will not attempt to re-identify that information except as required by law.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK, and similar laws)

Where those laws apply, we process personal data on these bases:

  • Contract — to provide the App you asked to use, including generating responses, accounts, and paid features you purchase
  • Legitimate interests — security, abuse prevention, rate limits, aggregate product improvement, and defending legal claims, in ways that do not override your rights
  • Legal obligation — tax, accounting, or lawful requests
  • Consent — where we ask for it (for example, in-app acceptance of this policy). You may withdraw consent without affecting processing that does not rely on consent

4. AI processing

Your inputs are sent to AI infrastructure so the App can generate a response in the moment. We route requests through an API gateway (currently OpenRouter) to model providers. Providers may include xAI, OpenAI, and other models we select over time. The specific model can change as we operate the service.

We do not use your conversations to train our own models. We configure paid API endpoints with no-training / no-retention options where the provider offers them. Providers may still process content briefly to deliver the completion and to monitor for abuse, as described in their own terms.

Human review of conversation content occurs only for safety, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or if you send it to us in a support request — not for marketing.

Metadata such as token counts and timestamps may be retained for performance, billing, and abuse prevention.

5. How we share information

We share limited information with processors who help us run the Services:

  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Apple and Google — app distribution and in-app purchases
  • RevenueCat — subscription status and purchase verification
  • OpenRouter and underlying model providers — to generate responses
  • Resend — transactional email (account, reset, deletion correspondence)
  • Professional advisers under confidentiality, if needed
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security
  • A successor, if we sell or reorganize the business, under appropriate safeguards

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the Services, fulfill subscriptions, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Conversation history for signed-in and Premium users is retained to support reflection until you delete it or we close the account. Guest technical data for rate limits is kept only as long as needed for that purpose.

You may request deletion in the App (Settings → account → Delete Account), by emailing support@quietlight.app, or via quietlight.app/delete-account.html (available after uninstall). Store subscriptions must be cancelled separately with Apple or Google. When you delete content or your account, we queue it for deletion. Removal from live systems typically completes within 30 days, except records we must keep for law, security, abuse prevention, or dispute resolution, and copies that may remain in encrypted backups until those backups rotate. The stores may retain billing proof under their own policies.

7. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, and industry-standard hosting. No system is fully secure. Transmission of reflections to AI providers is necessary for the App to work; we cannot guarantee absolute protection. You are responsible for protecting your password, limiting access to your devices, and signing out after use.

8. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, or under the digital-consent age in your country if higher (up to 16 in parts of the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have such data, we delete it. Parents or guardians: email support@quietlight.app. If you are under 13, do not register for or use the Services.

To report child sexual exploitation or suspected child sexual abuse material, email support@quietlight.app. We report suspected CSAM to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required.

Quiet Light is not crisis support. If you or someone else is in danger, contact local emergency services. In the United States, you can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information; to restrict or object to certain processing; to withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

California and certain other U.S. state residents may also have rights to know categories of personal information, to delete or correct it, and to opt out of “sale” or “sharing.” We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CPRA. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right-to-limit notice. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

Submit requests in the App, at delete-account.html, or by emailing support@quietlight.app. We will verify that you are the person the request is about (for example by confirming control of the account email) and respond within the time the law requires. Some information may be exempt, for example if we must keep it to comply with law or to establish or defend legal claims; if an exception applies we will say so. You may use an authorized agent as permitted by law; we may ask for written proof of that authority.

If we refuse a privacy request, you may appeal by emailing support@quietlight.app with enough detail to identify the original request and the reason for the appeal. We will respond within the time the law requires.

10. International transfers

We are based in the United States. Information is processed in the United States and in other countries where our providers operate. Those countries may not provide the same legal protections as your home country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by our providers (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

11. Do not track; automated decisions; links

The website does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals. We do not track you across third-party sites for advertising. AI responses are generated automatically for the purpose of the reflection you requested; they are not used to make legal or similarly significant decisions about you (credit, employment, insurance, or the like).

The Services may link to external sites (for example crisis resources, Apple, or Google). Those sites have their own terms and privacy policies. Read them before you submit information there.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The version number and “Last updated” date will change. Material changes will be posted here and, for the App, users may be asked to re-acknowledge the policy in-app. Continued use after an update applies to later collection; it does not retroactively change how we handled information already collected, except as the law allows.
